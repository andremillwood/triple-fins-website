About the program

Three fins. One standard.

Triple Fins Swim Program is built on three fundamentals — technique, endurance, and discipline. We develop young athletes through mentorship and training that sharpens stroke technique, builds endurance, and instils a strong, disciplined work ethic.

Using individual goal-setting and personalised training plans, we help swimmers reach their full potential — growing top-performing, well-rounded athletes in body and mind.
